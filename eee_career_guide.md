## Companies to Target for Electrical & Electronics Engineering (EEE) Students
*Not every company hires for the same skills. Your preparation should match the industry you want to enter.*

### 1. Power Generation & Transmission
* **What they do:** Generate electricity, operate substations, manage transmission lines, maintain power grids.
* **Top Companies:** NTPC, Power Grid Corporation of India (PGCIL), NHPC, SJVN, NLC India.
* **Skills Required:** Power Systems, Electrical Machines, Protection, Switchgear, Load Flow, Relay Coordination, ETAP, MATLAB.
* **Salary (Freshers):** ₹6–15 LPA (varies by company and role).

### 2. Power Equipment Manufacturers
* **What they do:** Manufacture transformers, motors, switchgear, protection relays, and substations.
* **Top Companies:** Schneider Electric, Siemens, ABB, Hitachi Energy, GE Vernova, CG Power and Industrial Solutions, Kirloskar Electric.
* **Skills Required:** ETAP, AutoCAD Electrical, PLC, Protection, Electrical Machines, Industrial Wiring, Circuit Design.
* **Salary:** ₹4–12 LPA.

### 3. Industrial Automation
* **What they do:** Factories are becoming smarter with automation.
* **Companies:** Rockwell Automation, Honeywell, Mitsubishi Electric, Emerson, Yokogawa.
* **Learn:** PLC, SCADA, HMI, Industrial Networking, Sensors, VFDs.

### 4. Electric Vehicle (EV) Industry
* **What they do:** One of the fastest-growing sectors for EEE graduates.
* **Companies:** Tesla, BYD, Tata Motors, Mahindra Electric, Ather Energy, Ola Electric.
* **Learn:** Battery Management System (BMS), Motor Drives, Embedded Systems, MATLAB, Simulink, CAN Communication, Battery Technology.

### 5. Renewable Energy
* **What they do:** Demand is increasing worldwide due to the shift toward clean energy.
* **Companies:** Adani Green Energy, Tata Power Renewable Energy, ReNew, Suzlon, Vestas.
* **Learn:** Solar PV Design, Wind Energy, Power Electronics, Grid Integration, ETAP, PVSyst (optional).

### 6. Oil & Gas / EPC Companies
* **What they do:** Electrical engineers are needed for designing and maintaining electrical systems in large industrial plants.
* **Companies:** Larsen & Toubro (L&T), Technip Energies, Fluor, Petrofac.
* **Learn:** AutoCAD Electrical, Electrical Design, Cable Sizing, Earthing, Lighting Design.

### 7. Semiconductor Industry
* **What they do:** An exciting option if you're interested in electronics and chip design.
* **Companies:** Texas Instruments, Intel, NXP Semiconductors, Infineon Technologies, Analog Devices, Micron Technology.
* **Learn:** Embedded C, Digital Electronics, VLSI Basics, PCB Design, Microcontrollers.

### 8. Government Jobs
* **What they do:** Many students prepare for public-sector roles because of job stability and structured career growth.
* **Organizations:** BHEL, Indian Railways, DRDO, ISRO, NPCIL.
* **Preparation:** GATE, Electrical Core Subjects, Aptitude, Technical Interviews.

## Recommended Career Roadmap

* **If you like Power Systems:**
  * Learn ETAP, MATLAB, Protection, and Power System Analysis.
  * Target: PGCIL, NTPC, Siemens, ABB, Schneider Electric.
* **If you like Automation:**
  * Learn PLC, SCADA, HMI, and Industrial Networking.
  * Target: Rockwell Automation, Honeywell, Emerson.

  * Learn BMS, Motor Drives, Embedded Systems, and Simulink.
  * Target: Tesla, Tata Motors, Ather Energy, BYD.

  * Learn Solar PV Design, ETAP, and Grid Integration.

* **If you like Electronics:**
  * Learn Embedded C, Digital Electronics, and PCB Design.
  * Target: Texas Instruments, NXP Semiconductors, Infineon Technologies.

## Final Advice for Juniors
Don't apply to every company with the same resume. Choose one domain by your third year, build the relevant software skills, complete 2–3 domain-specific projects, earn a few recognized certifications, and tailor your resume to that field. A focused profile is usually much more attractive to recruiters than a resume with unrelated skills.
